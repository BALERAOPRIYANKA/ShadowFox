## Objective:
Conduct advanced fielding performance analysis using ball-by-ball data from a T20 World Cup match. 
## Project Description:
This project analyzes the fielding performances of selected players during the T20 World Cup 2026 Final between India and New Zealand. 
The analysis focuses on fielding actions, player positions, catches, dropped catches, runs saved or conceded, and overall fielding performance. 
## Data Collection:
The data was collected from match fielding events and organized into a structured spreadsheet.
The dataset includes: 
Match number 
Innings 
Team 
Fielder 
Ball count 
Fielding position
